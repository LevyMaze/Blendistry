# Soft Skills Developers Undervalue

Many developers think their job is just code. But the best engineers excel at **soft skills** too.

## Why They Matter

- Communicating trade-offs saves projects.  
- Negotiating deadlines avoids burnout.  
- Empathy makes teams stronger.  

## Commonly Undervalued Skills

- **Explaining complex ideas simply.**  
- **Asking clarifying questions.**  
- **Writing documentation others can follow.**  

## Example: Explaining Code to Non-Devs

Instead of:  
> “We need to refactor because of technical debt.”  

Say:  
> “If we don’t clean this up, new features will take 3x longer.”  

## Quick Checklist

- ✅ Practice active listening.  
- ✅ Translate tech into business value.  
- ✅ Write clear docs and PRs.  

## What To Do Next

Spend as much time improving communication as you do coding. It pays dividends in career growth.
